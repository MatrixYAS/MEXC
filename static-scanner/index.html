All settings are client-side: they are stored in this browser only and applied the moment you restart the scan. The scanner connects to MEXC's public market-data endpoints — no API key is required. Net yield = gross gap − (N × taker fee) − slippage buffer, where N is 3 legs (triangle) or 4 legs (quadrangle). Optimal trade size is binary-searched so every leg fills while net yield stays at or above your minimum. Profit shown = optimal size × net yield at that exact size — small capacity + high yield is how the market pays out on thin books.
