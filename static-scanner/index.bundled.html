All settings are client-side: stored in this browser only, applied on restart. The scanner connects to MEXC's public market-data endpoints — no API key required. Net yield = gross gap − (N × taker fee) − slippage buffer, with N = 2, 4, 5 or 6 legs. Slippage is the weighted-fill average per leg walked through real depth, in quote-USD so both directions share one scale. Optimal size = largest size that still fills every leg with net yield ≥ your minimum, binary-searched from live books.
